The Path to Ordination

A sacred journey of preparation, discernment, and commitment. Explore the rigorous and deeply spiritual process of becoming an ordained minister within our tradition.

Journey of Discernment

Our four-stage process is designed to support you academically, spiritually, and practically as you prepare for a lifetime of ministry.

1
description

Application

Submit your initial dossier, including references, transcripts, and a comprehensive statement of faith and calling.

2
diversity_3

Mentorship

Engage in a 12-month guided mentorship program with a senior pastoral leader, focusing on practical ministry skills.

3
gavel

Examination

Appear before the ordination council for a comprehensive theological, biblical, and pastoral examination.

4
celebration

Ceremony

The culminating service of laying on of hands, formally recognizing and commissioning you for pastoral ministry.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the ordination process typically take? expand_more
The process generally takes between 18 to 24 months, depending on the speed at which you complete the mentorship requirements and document submissions.
Can I apply before completing my M.Div.? expand_more
You may begin the initial application process during your final semester of your M.Div. program, but formal examination cannot occur until degree conferral.
Is there an application fee? expand_more
Yes, there is a non-refundable $150 processing fee required upon submission of your initial dossier to cover administrative and background check costs.

Need Guidance on Your Journey?

Our ordination advisors are available to discuss your specific calling, review your eligibility, and answer any questions you have about the process.
